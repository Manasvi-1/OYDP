1. Executive decision and product definition
PropertyTrust OS is a single property intelligence, collaboration and lifecycle platform supporting buyers, sellers,
property owners, brokers, qualified lawyers, developers, learners and authorized institutional consumers. It
combines a property identity and evidence record, context-aware guides, a transaction workspace, ongoing
ownership operations, geospatial data, comparative valuation, conceptual development design, feasibility
calculations, a controlled marketplace, a time-versioned spatial view and a restricted institutional API.
The key strategic correction from v1 is breadth of engine availability at GA rather than deferring entire modules.
Each engine starts with a deliberately narrow, well-tested capability. Advanced reconstruction, nationwide realtime registry integrations, bespoke generative CAD, predictive prices, autonomous registration and cryptographic
title-verdict claims are outside the first release.
Company promise: Help people make informed property decisions and coordinate tasks with linked evidence,
clear uncertainties, professional accountability and transferable records. Never promise guaranteed legal title,
guaranteed development approvals, guaranteed investment returns, or government affiliation.

2. Consolidated sources and evidence boundaries
SpatialLedger — The Universal Spatial Intelligence & Title Infrastructure: proposes multi-dimensional
property indexing, links among legal/physical/economic data, advanced spatial records, institutional APIs and
privacy-preserving verification. Adopt spatial identity, time-versioned GIS layers and documented sources; do not
adopt unvalidated claims of fraud elimination, universal millimeter-scale spatial hashes, sub-second conclusive title
opinions or underwriting in seconds. A hash authenticates a committed data object, not the underlying property
rights.
PropertyPulse — Real Estate Startup Opportunity Research (October 2026): proposes a private living passport
for owners, document organization, jurisdiction-aware actions, evidence and source links, professional handoff,
transfer packs, and a Bengaluru-first post-purchase entry point. Incorporate these as PropertyTrust Ownership and
Actions. Respect its warning that low-frequency individual usage and difficult government access may constrain
monetization.
Earlier PropertyTrust OS blueprint: includes buyers, sellers, professional roles, the Deal OS, educational
guidance, a marketplace, investment intelligence, valuation, developer feasibility and conceptual designs. These
remain in one integrated product, not independent startups.
Research status: Product innovation, willingness to pay, lawful source access, patentability, competitive edge,
institutional adoption and profitability remain hypotheses. Resolve through user research and signed
commercial/data agreements rather than treating the source theses as proofs.

4. Unified domain architecture
The primary domain entity is a Property, which may link to official parcel numbers, building identifiers and unit
identifiers, each tagged by jurisdiction, source, period and confidence. A property may have many documents,
relationships, evidence assertions, risks, transactions, market comparables, plan scenarios, maintenance events
and owners/organizations with different lawful access grants.
Core domain aggregates: PropertyIdentity, ParcelGeometry, PropertyUnit, EvidenceObject, EvidenceAssertion,
SourcePublication, ProvenanceEvent, DiligenceCase, ParticipantGrant, ReviewOpinion, OwnershipPassport,
ActionTask, MarketplaceListing, Inquiry, ComparableTransaction, ValuationRun, LayoutScenario, CostScenario,
TwinSnapshot, Lesson, Course, InstitutionalAccessGrant and SecurityAuditEvent.
Standard evidence attributes: source_kind, source_name, jurisdiction, source_reference, retrieved_at, valid_from, valid_to,
record_date, observed_date, effective_date, original_object_hash, extraction_model, page_coordinate, extraction_confidence,
human_review_status, disclosure_scope and retention_basis as applicable. Distinguish a user claim, a downloaded record,

a professional conclusion and an official confirmed status.
Shared state logic: cases transition through created → evidence gathering → professional review → negotiation →
conditions → official completion recorded → archived, with cancellation and reopening branches. Approvals and
evidentiary statements are never silently overwritten: publish new versions and link to preceding versions.

Data isolation: Every private resource belongs to a specific organization/ownership context and a property, then

5. The 13 launch engines: functional acceptance contracts
Engine 01 — Property Identity & Evidence Graph
GA minimum: create/search a property from user-provided fields; link available official identifiers and
approximate coordinates; upload and categorize source documents; label claims as verified, conflicting, pending or
unknown only after an accountable process; show provenance relationships and duplicate-candidate alerts. Permit
a source-supported correction workflow. Acceptance: no property marked legally verified from owner self-report;
ambiguous address/parcel matches flag manual review; each claim has evidence and access controls.

Engine 02 — AI Buyer, Seller, Owner & Developer Guide
GA minimum: persona- and location-aware procedures, explanations, question/answer workflow grounded in a
versioned approved source corpus, clear unknowns, user-specific task generation and professional escalation. A
general-language-model response is never treated as authoritative. Acceptance: test at least 100 evaluated tasks and
adversarial prompts per supported jurisdiction and role; all consequential procedural guidance cites applicable
sources; unsupported legal conclusions abstain.

Engine 03 — Transaction & Legal Workspace
GA minimum: create an invited case, manage parties, issue permissions, request and review documents, record
buyer questions and seller replies, track offers/status and professional review outcomes, maintain append-only
activity events. Acceptance: cross-party revocation, audit replay and task ownership tested; no transfer of title is
claimed until external official completion evidence is recorded.

Engine 04 — PropertyPulse Living Property Passport
GA minimum: owner-controlled encrypted files, dated property events, loan/insurance/maintenance metadata,
document retention controls and owner-authorized signed export manifest. Acceptance: no original-document
disclosure in public profiles; export and deletion workflows validated; files verifiable against stored hashes.

Engine 05 — Ownership Action Engine
GA minimum: customizable and rule-suggested action lists, jurisdiction/source-backed explanations, due dates, task
assignment, completion evidence and opt-in email notifications. Acceptance: no unsupported mandatory claims, no
duplicates for a single event, task notifications respect opt-in and access revocation.

Engine 06 — Real Estate Academy
GA minimum: structured introductory buyer, seller, owner, developer and investing learning tracks; quizzes,
progress tracking and source-dated materials, with integrated simulations using synthetic properties. Acceptance:
course facts reviewed; certificates only describe course completion and never imply professional or governmental
accreditation.

Engine 07 — SpatialLedger GIS Intelligence
GA minimum: map-enabled property records, coordinates, uploaded permissible survey geometry, parcel overlays
for licensed/authorized data, basic distance/context displays and spatial-source provenance. Acceptance:
approximate pins never masquerade as surveyed cadastral boundaries; coordinate reference systems recorded and
boundaries source-labeled.

Engine 08 — Price & Market Intelligence
GA minimum: manual or licensed comparable transactions, normalized area units, simple comparable-based
ranges, historical price facts where licensed, filters on sale date/area/asset class, and warnings about sparse/biased
data. Acceptance: never label listing asks as completed sale prices; source type and vintage visible; no fake
precision for insufficient evidence.

Engine 09 — AI Layout & Architecture Concept Studio
GA minimum: allow land dimensions and allowed design constraints to be entered; generate simple conceptual
plot/building massing alternatives with approximate area breakdown, road/amenity assumptions and
downloadable scenario summary. Acceptance: concepts visibly stamped 'not surveyed, engineered or approved';
professional review required before statutory use.

Engine 10 — Development & Business Feasibility
GA minimum: input-driven capex, infrastructure, saleable-area and asking/realized-price assumptions; compute
break-even, operating margin, downside sensitivities and project scenarios. Basic local-business use comparison
permitted with disclosed assumptions. Acceptance: calculations tested and transparent; no unsupported
guaranteed ROI or price-appreciation claims.

Engine 11 — Marketplace & Professional Network
GA minimum: voluntary property summary listing, appropriate moderation, owner-controlled enquiries, qualified
professional profiles with verifiable eligibility status, invited engagements and disclosed referral relationships.
Acceptance: seller permission documented; restricted evidence never public; reporting/moderation and anti-spam
controls present.

Engine 12 — Time-Versioned Spatial Twin
GA minimum: owner/partner-provided building massing observations and dated geometry versions with a basic
2.5D/3D view and explicit observation provenance. A history slider compares versions. Acceptance: building
changes not inferred as facts from unsourced estimates; no claim of live LiDAR, millimeter precision or complete
subsurface rights.

Engine 13 — Institutional API & Evidence Attestations
GA minimum: contracted read-only, consent-scoped access to specified fields, tenant-bound credentials, audit
events, API versioning, pagination and response digests/signatures to demonstrate payload integrity. Acceptance:
no bulk data access outside grants; revocation tested; no ZK proof or legal title certification promised.

7. Production reference stack and implementation separation
Web: Next.js + TypeScript, component design system, accessible responsive UI, localization keys. Backend: Python
FastAPI, modular domain services and strict API models. Primary database: PostgreSQL with PostGIS; use rowlevel security and typed ORM/migrations. Files: encrypted S3-compatible object storage, malware scanning, MIME
verification, short-lived signed URLs, content-security controls. Identity: audited managed OIDC, MFA for sensitive
roles, organization invites, explicit grants. Async tasks: queue/worker, rate controls, retries and dead-letter
monitoring. Search: PostgreSQL initially; dedicated search only after profiling. GIS: PostGIS + authorized mapping
layers; coordinate-source fidelity. Audit: append-only events, integrity hashes, a separate immutable log store. AI:
approved-source retrieval, citation chain, tool allowlist, prompt-injection defenses, output schema validation and
evaluation harness. Infrastructure: EU/India cloud availability evaluated for legal/data considerations, CI/CD,
ephemeral preview environments, IaC and isolated dev/staging/prod.
A monolith with domain modules is preferred until teams and scale justify separate services. Do not create 13
microservices at launch. Separation of authorization/evidence from agent output is mandatory.

8. AI architecture, responsibility and evaluations
Use AI for explanations, semantic search, draft extraction, document comparisons and procedural task suggestions.
Use deterministic calculators for prices and project margins, deterministic policy checks for allowable actions, and
accountable professionals for licensed legal/technical conclusions. Uploaded documents are untrusted inputs that
can contain malicious instructions; models must never execute tool operations because a document requests them.
An AI answer should include: question, supported factual statements with source references, rule/jurisdiction
version, evidence gaps, actionable next steps, explicit limitations, and qualified reviewer escalation. Unknown or
inaccessible status is displayed as unknown, never passed off as clean/verified.
Evaluation gates: measure source relevance, citation correctness, harmful overclaims, evidence extraction
precision/recall, duplicate property-matching errors, hallucination, language parity, access-control bypass via
prompts, calculator accuracy and geographic errors. Require human sign-off for any launch context claiming
regulatory compliance. Log evaluated model/prompt versions without exposing protected user data in general
telemetry.

9. Karnataka-first legal and data procurement plan
Validate separately with local counsel and official providers: property registration and EC services; municipal
khata/e-Aasthi systems; RERA records; revenue and land-use rules; planning authorities; court and charge searches;
map/parcel services; and permitted transaction price evidence. Capture service availability, geographic coverage,
lawful access modality, rate limits, licensing, fees, publication permissions, and retention restrictions before
making 'live verified' claims.

10. Security, privacy, operations and reliability gates
Launch-blocking controls: verified identity and MFA; strict per-object authorization and tenancy isolation;
encryption at rest/in transit; file malware and active-content checks; data minimization; clear
privacy/retention/deletion/export policies; user consent, revocation and professional invitations; audit logs and
secure secrets; infrastructure hardening; cloud data backups and independently tested restore; vulnerability
management; abuse prevention, rate limiting and monitored alerts; staging penetration test with critical/high
findings resolved or formally accepted by accountable security leadership; incident handling and breach processes;
independent legal review of marketing and title claims.
Operational runbooks: onboarding a law firm, customer support escalation, missing/inaccurate source records,
disputed ownership, document removal, compromised session, infrastructure outage, database recovery,
emergency permission revocation and erroneous AI guidance.
SLO targets to validate with pilots: 99.5% monthly availability for the early GA core, P95 read request under 1
second for non-computational endpoints at expected load, 100% of sensitive file access auditable, and zero known
critical open vulnerabilities at public launch. Targets are engineering objectives, not achieved metrics.

12. Commercialization, staffing and budget
First target users: Bengaluru law practices, credible transaction advisers and brokerages coordinating resale
apartment cases; include opt-in sellers and owners. Adjacent revenue: developer feasibility and professional GIS
planning support where permitted; discounted paid training; per-case property dossiers; professional
subscriptions; institutional integrations later. A first customer paying for reduced admin effort is better proof than
thousands of free registrations.
Indicative team: founder/product lead, tech lead, 2–3 backend/data engineers, 2 frontend engineers, 1
geospatial/quant specialist, 1 AI evaluation/document engineer, part-time DevSecOps, QA, privacy/legal counsel and
partner operations. Some roles may be contracted. Founder-led prototype can cost far less but will not replace
independent security, legal and geospatial expertise.
Capital planning bands, not supplier quotes: 4–8 weeks of self-funded development to validate demo is distinct
from GA. For 13-engine GA, provisionally plan a 9–15 month execution program and approximately ₹1.0–3.5
crore in aggregate people, cloud, legal, security, data licensing and pilot operations, with very high uncertainty. The
37–44 week schedule above is a target with strong staffing and limited geographic/data scope; realistic dates depend
on discovery. Cash budget can fall materially with founder execution and firm partnerships, but data rights and
legal verification cannot be skipped.
Business KPIs: partner commitments, invited deal activation, workflows completed, document review effort, cost
per case, paid renewal, process cycle time, false confidence flags, customer data incident rate, successful export and
referral rate. Do not use gross property GMV as the same thing as recognized company revenue.

14. Definition of done for an engine
Feature works in deployed staging and mobile-responsive web UI; API and storage contracts documented;
authorization checked server-side; expected/negative-path tests pass; data quality and evidence source surfaced;
localization/accessible errors checked; monitoring and audit defined; security and privacy review completed;
support and rollback path exists; user documentation and limits are clear. For externally authoritative data, actual
permissions and source provenance must be recorded.

15. What has been implemented as of this deliverable
The accompanying development repository contains 13 functional demonstration API flows and a local web
control panel: property identity/evidence references, educational procedure steps, deal creation, passport events
and manifest, tasks, Academy quiz, basic GIS point, manual comparable estimation, conceptual plot grid,
deterministic feasibility, listings/enquiries, dated building snapshots and a minimal institutional evidence
summary. The FastAPI preview uses SQLite, a single development token and synthetic owner context.
The repository is NOT production-ready and explicitly fails if `APP_ENV=production`. It lacks audited
OIDC/MFA, multi-tenant policy enforcement, encrypted document blob service, scoped signed consent, real
professional review, licensed market feeds, geospatial parcel validation, a full 3D viewer, official regulatory
integrations, full source-grounded LLM operations, and independent production acceptance evidence. Those are
scheduled engineering deliverables rather than hidden assumptions.

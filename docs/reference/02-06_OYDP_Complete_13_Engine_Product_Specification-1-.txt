01. Product invariant
All thirteen engines must each deliver a genuine, accessible, secured and evaluated user workflow at general availability.
Advanced functionality within an engine may be disabled; a placeholder dashboard, synthetic-only production API or mocked
official integration does not satisfy release. Every claim carries source attribution or UNKNOWN.

03. Shared dependencies and launch wiring
Engines 01, identity/consent and audited evidence are foundational. Engines 03–05 share case-to-ownership transitions; 02 and
06 share approved rule revisions; 07–10 and 12 share coordinate and geometry source lineage; 11 only publishes a redacted
consent-approved subset; 13 never escapes field-level partner grants. The 13 engineering PDFs supply the required A–AM
contracts, negative test cases and API proposals.
¹ The listed engineer-weeks are independent module sizing heuristics before integration, localization, legal review and
organizational overhead. They are not estimates of elapsed schedule or vendor quotations.

04. Cross-engine acceptance sequence
Synthetic property -> identity with ambiguous-match handling -> quarantine and upload -> invited buyer/seller deal ->
lawyer-scoped review -> owner passport and reminders -> approved-source adviser and Academy -> geospatial context ->
manual comparable valuation -> conceptual layout -> transparent feasibility -> opt-in listing -> versioned 3D massing ->
consented partner read-only response. A single staged journey must demonstrate explicit role grants and revocation at each
step.

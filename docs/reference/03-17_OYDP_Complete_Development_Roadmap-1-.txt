01. Planning assumptions / staffing reality
An appropriately staffed team can target 44–52+ weeks with design, engineering, professional verification and controlled paid
pilots in parallel. A founder-led small team could require approximately 18–30+ months; a medium team 12–20+ months, and
well-funded 9–15+ months. These are non-binding planning hypotheses, not a promise of throughput; official dataset access can
dominate the critical path [S01].

03. Dependencies and parallel work
Critical path: legal/official data rights + tenant auth -> canonical property identity -> scanned evidence -> source-grounded AI
and invited deal -> all 13 useful UI flows -> geographic/localization tests -> paid pilots -> penetration test and launch signatures.
Academy curriculum, deterministic financial engine and frontend design system can start in parallel after schemas. GIS overlays
and valuation depend on lawful sources; a missing licensed dataset must not be replaced by synthetic production claims.

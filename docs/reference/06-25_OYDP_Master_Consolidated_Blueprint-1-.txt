01. Controlled decision summary
Project: OYDP — Own Your Dream Property; master control ID OYDP-MASTER-001; version 1.0 research/engineering baseline
(16 October 2026). Scope: One platform, 13 bounded engines in GA; Bengaluru Urban, Bengaluru Rural, remaining Karnataka,
Hyderabad in launch program with geography-specific availability and clear unsupported-source disclosures. Public name
PropertyTrust OS is retired; original concepts PropertyPulse and SpatialLedger remain modules.

Figure: DFD_LEVEL_1. Editable source: 13_DIAGRAMS/DFD_LEVEL_1.mmd

02. Mission and realistic competitive claim
OYDP — Own Your Dream Property — is a proposed India-first property decision and lifecycle platform that unifies evidence
identity, buyer and seller guidance, invited diligence, ownership records, location intelligence, transparent valuation,
development concepts, feasibility and education. The project will sell workflow utility to lawyers, brokers and property advisers
while retaining a privacy-preserving consumer interface. The 13 engines are bounded first-release commitments, not thirteen
companies or thirteen microservices.
The category is competitive: marketplaces already sell discovery and legal services [S15,S16]; industry data providers and AEC
tools provide intelligence and site optioneering [S17,S19,S20]. OYDP's differentiation must be measured through documented
source lineage, revocable permissions and a persistent property-specific collaborative workspace—not claimed merely through
an AI chat interface.

05. Unified architecture
A modular FastAPI monolith and Next.js front end manage property UID and evidence graph, granted user roles, source dated
jurisdiction rules, PostGIS geometry, object storage and independent audit. AI is grounded, not authoritative; economic
calculators are reproducible; lawyers and architects retain professionally regulated decisions. This repository of documents
includes 13 deep A–AM engineering contracts and editable diagrams, not a production application.

06. Commercial and release approach
Paying professional coordination pilots come first; region and property type coverage only open when rules, source access,
licensed data, localized review and support are validated. Use workbook scenarios instead of conflating house transaction value
with OYDP software revenue. The estimate ₹1.0–3.5 crore for a staffed, legally reviewed 13-engine GA comes from the prior
internal source as a tentative planning band [S01] and requires quotes.

07. Required eight release gates
• 13 actual E2E customer-facing engine journeys and audited source limits
• Data rights / signed local legal source review
• External security assessment and cross-tenant negative tests
• Consent, deletion, export and revocation evidence
• Grounded AI evaluation and finance/GIS calculator accuracy
• Operational logs, monitoring, backups and restore drills
• Independent paid demand and viable unit economics
• Dated owner approvals from engineering, security, legal, operations and product
